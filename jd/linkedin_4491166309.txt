Job Description Job Summary** We are seeking an experienced and highly skilled VP, Data Engineer to lead and contribute to the design, development, and maintenance of robust and scalable data solutions for UOB Asset Management. This role will be crucial in building and optimizing data pipelines, data warehouses, and data lakes to support advanced analytics, reporting, and business intelligence initiatives within the asset management domain. Job Responsibilities** Lead the design, development, and implementation of highly scalable, reliable, and efficient data pipelines and ETL processes using various data integration tools and technologies. Architect and optimize data warehouses, data marts, and data lakes to meet the evolving analytical and reporting needs of UOB Asset Management. Collaborate closely with business stakeholders, data scientists, and other technology teams to understand data requirements and translate them into technical solutions. Ensure data quality, integrity, and security across all data platforms and processes. Implement and maintain data governance policies and procedures. Perform performance tuning and optimization of data systems and queries. Evaluate and recommend new data technologies and tools to enhance our data capabilities. Mentor junior data engineers and contribute to best practices for data engineering within the team. Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, and architectural decisions. Stay abreast of industry trends and advancements in data engineering and financial technology. Job Qualifications** Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related quantitative field. Minimum of 8 years of experience in data engineering, with a strong focus on building and managing large-scale data platforms. Proven experience in designing and implementing data warehouses, data lakes, and ETL/ELT processes. Expertise in SQL and experience with various relational and NoSQL databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, Cassandra). Proficiency in at least one programming language commonly used in data engineering (e.g., Python, Scala, Java). Hands-on experience with cloud-based data platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud Platform) and their data services (e.g., S3, Redshift, BigQuery). Experience with big data technologies such as Spark, Hadoop, Kafka. Solid understanding of data modeling techniques (dimensional modeling, Kimball, Inmon). Familiarity with data governance, data quality, and data security principles. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen attention to detail. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to effectively collaborate with diverse teams and stakeholders.
