  Position Overview   We are seeking a skilled Data Engineer (Analytics Engineering) to design, develop and maintain data models, curated datasets and pipelines that support business intelligence, analytics and data science initiatives.  The successful candidate will work closely with business stakeholders to understand data requirements, translate business needs into technical solutions and deliver reliable, scalable and reusable data products.  The technology stack primarily includes Databricks and AWS.   Key Responsibilities    Engage with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into data models, datasets and pipelines.  Design and develop data models and transformation pipelines to integrate, clean and curate data from multiple sources.  Build and maintain scalable, reliable and reusable data pipelines using Databricks, Apache Spark and SQL.  Develop optimised datasets for reporting, business intelligence, analytics and data science applications.  Implement data quality checks, validation and reconciliation processes to ensure data accuracy and consistency.  Monitor, troubleshoot and optimise data pipeline performance and resolve operational issues.  Maintain documentation for data models, datasets, transformations and pipeline dependencies.  Collaborate with data engineers, analysts and data scientists to deliver solutions aligned with business requirements.    Requirements    Strong proficiency in SQL, relational databases and data warehousing.  Solid experience in data modelling, including dimensional modelling and analytical dataset design.  Proficiency in Python for data processing and pipeline development.  Hands-on experience with Databricks, Apache Spark and Delta Lake.  Experience designing and developing ETL/ELT pipelines and data transformation workflows.  Familiarity with AWS data services and cloud-based data platforms.  Strong stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to translate business requirements into technical solutions.  Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Engineering or a related discipline.    Preferred Qualifications    Experience with Git, CI/CD and data pipeline orchestration.  Familiarity with Databricks Workflows and Unity Catalog.  Experience developing data products for business intelligence and self-service analytics.  Knowledge of data governance, access control and data quality best practices.  Experience working in government or highly regulated environments.
