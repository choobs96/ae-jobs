Data Engineer (Cloud Analytics) (JD#11353)
Job Summary
 Seeking a mid-level Analytics Engineer / Data Engineer with strong SQL, Python, data modelling, dbt, orchestration, and modern software engineering skills to support data platform development and modernization.
 Mandatory Skill-set
 Must have 3–6 years of relevant Data/Analytics Engineering experience;
 Must have strong hands-on SQL and good Python knowledge;
 Experience in dimensional data modelling and data marts;
 Hands-on experience with dbt or equivalent transformation frameworks;
 Experience with Airflow or equivalent orchestration tools;
 Understanding of semantic layers and business metrics;
 Knowledge of Git, code reviews, testing, and CI/CD;
 Strong understanding of ETL/ELT and data warehouse concepts;
 Ability to troubleshoot data and pipeline issues independently.
 Desired Skill-set
 Experience with GCP, including BigQuery, Cloud Composer, and Cloud Storage;
 Exposure to .NET business logic, BI/reporting tools, and analytical data consumption.
 Responsibilities
 Support development and modernization of data and analytics platforms;
 Develop and maintain SQL and Python-based data solutions;
 Build dimensional models, fact tables, dimension tables, and data marts;
 Develop data transformations using dbt or equivalent frameworks;
 Build and manage workflows using Airflow or equivalent tools;
 Implement reusable business logic, metrics, and semantic-layer concepts;
 Troubleshoot data and pipeline issues and follow software engineering best practices.
