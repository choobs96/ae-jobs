 iCORE (Integrated Core Research Operations & Excellence) is a research entity within A*STAR, restructured from the Research Support Centre (RSC). iCORE primarily supports the Biomedical Research Council (BMRC) cluster, while the Strategic Initiatives team operates across the broader A*STAR organisation, driving digital transformation, data infrastructure, and process innovation to accelerate scientific discovery and institutional effectiveness.   Role Overview   We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to join the Strategic Initiatives team at iCORE. This is a hands-on, end-to-end ownership role. On a typical day, the engineer will be designing and maintaining data pipelines, managing and optimising the Snowflake data warehouse, enforcing data quality across ingestion and transformation layers, and managing access controls — while also engaging with research institute counterparts, ITSS, and external vendors to align on integration requirements and data standards.  The role supports iCORE’s two active data workstreams:   the iCORE dashboards, including Asset Lifecycle Management (ALCM) and platform performance metrics dashboard built on SAP ERP, Snowflake, and Power BI;  and a forthcoming lab digitalisation initiative to build a centralised research data hub consolidating data from disparate laboratory systems (LIMs, ELN, integrated platform, etc..) across BMRC research institutes.   This role works in close partnership with the Business Data Analyst, who leads stakeholder requirement gathering, analytics, and reporting. The Data Engineer focuses on the infrastructure layer — ingestion, transformation, modelling, and pipeline reliability — ensuring the analyst has clean, consistent, and trusted data to work with.    Key Responsibilities      Data Pipeline Development & Integration     Own and maintain data pipeline infrastructure across iCORE’s data workstreams, including pipelines supporting iCORE dashboards built on SAP ERP, Snowflake, and Power BI.  Build and maintain API integrations and ETL/ELT pipelines to ingest data from heterogeneous source systems — including SAP ERP, OEM equipment software, and laboratory systems — in a reliable and scalable manner.  Lead the migration of data transformation logic from Power BI (Power Query / DAX) into Snowflake, implementing scalable, version-controlled SQL transformations using best practices (e.g. dbt or equivalent).  Maintain and improve pipeline orchestration, scheduling, and alerting to ensure data is accurate and up to date.  Manage pipeline code using version control (Git), enforce code review practices, and where applicable implement automated testing and deployment of pipeline changes.  Collaborate with the Business Data Analyst to validate transformed data, resolve discrepancies, and ensure the semantic layer meets reporting requirements.    2. Data Modelling & Warehouse Management    Establish and enforce data modelling standards (e.g. layered architecture with raw, staged, and mart layers) to ensure the Snowflake warehouse remains maintainable and scalable over time.  Own the design and ongoing optimisation of the Snowflake data warehouse, including table structures, clustering strategies, materialised views, and partitioning to ensure query performance and cost efficiency.  Define and enforce data schemas, naming conventions, and field-level standards across integrated systems to ensure data consistency and interoperability.  Monitor and manage Snowflake compute and storage costs, implementing best practices to optimise resource usage as data volumes and pipeline complexity grow.    3. Data Quality & Observability    Implement automated data quality checks across ingestion and transformation layers to catch anomalies, schema drift, and data inconsistencies before they surface in downstream dashboards or reports.  Establish pipeline monitoring, alerting, and logging practices to ensure failures or data quality issues are detected and resolved proactively.  Define and track data quality metrics (e.g. completeness, accuracy, timeliness) for key data domains, working with the Business Data Analyst to validate outputs against business expectations.    4. Access Control & Data Security    Manage role-based access control (RBAC) within Snowflake, ensuring data access is appropriately scoped by user role, team, and data sensitivity.  Work with ITSS and compliance stakeholders to ensure data pipelines and storage practices align with A*STAR’s data governance policies and relevant data protection requirements.  Apply appropriate controls for sensitive research and equipment data ingested from BMRC research institutes and OEM systems.    5. Cross-Functional Engagement    Engage proactively with external vendors and ITSS to align on integration requirements, data formats, and API specifications.  Coordinate with counterparts across BMRC research institutes to support requirement validation and integration outputs, working alongside the Business Data Analyst who leads stakeholder requirement gathering.  Represent iCORE Strategic Initiatives in cross-RI and A*STAR-wide technical working groups related to data infrastructure and digitalisation.  Maintain pipeline documentation, field mapping specifications, and data dictionaries at a level sufficient for team maintainability and onboarding — with the expectation that dedicated data governance resources may be introduced in future. Governance & Documentation    Develop and maintain data governance artefacts including data dictionaries, lineage diagrams, and integration specifications.
