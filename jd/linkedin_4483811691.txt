Get To Know The Role We are looking for a Data Infrastructure Engineer to own the data backbone of our robotics programme. Our fleet produces a large, continuous and unforgiving stream of data — sensor recordings, telemetry, autonomy events, and operational and delivery records — and almost every decision the team makes depends on that data arriving complete, on time and demonstrably correct. You will own the pipelines that move it, the validation and monitoring that prove it is trustworthy, the dashboards that engineering, operations and data science work from, and the integration of our robotics data and ML workloads onto Grab's existing data and ML platforms, so the team inherits Grab-scale tooling instead of rebuilding it. This is an ownership role over an established and growing footprint: you will take on existing pipelines, raise their reliability, and shape where the platform goes next. The day-to-day activities Design, build and operate batch and streaming pipelines for robot telemetry, sensor recordings, autonomy events and operational data. Implement data quality and validation frameworks — schema enforcement, completeness and freshness checks, anomaly detection — with meaningful alerting when data breaks. Build observability for the pipelines themselves: throughput, lag, cost, failure modes and clear on-call signals. Build and maintain dashboards and curated, self-serve datasets for engineering, data science and fleet operations. Integrate robotics data and ML workloads onto Grab's central data and ML platforms — lakehouse, orchestration, feature and model tooling — rather than maintaining parallel stacks. Design storage, indexing and retention for high-volume sensor data (ROS 2 / MCAP recordings, video, point clouds), balancing accessibility against cost. Partner with autonomy, perception and ML engineers to define metrics, and make training and evaluation datasets reproducible and versioned. Own data access, governance, retention and compliance across the regions we operate in. Drive down platform cost through partitioning, storage tiering, lifecycle policies and compute right-sizing. Document pipelines and datasets so that consumers can self-serve without a hand-off conversation. If you want your work measured by whether engineers and data scientists can trust and reach their data, and you enjoy making a data platform boringly reliable, we'd love to hear from you. Please apply with your resume and a brief note describing a data quality or pipeline reliability problem you owned: how you detected it, what you changed, and how you knew it stayed fixed. Qualifications Technical must-haves Strong Python and SQL, with the ability to write production-grade data services rather than one-off scripts. Hands-on experience building and operating both batch and streaming pipelines (Spark, Flink, Kafka or equivalents). Workflow orchestration at production scale (Airflow, Dagster, Argo Workflows or similar). Data warehouse and lakehouse experience: open table formats (Iceberg, Delta, Hudi), partitioning strategy, and query engines (Presto/Trino, Spark SQL, BigQuery or equivalent). Demonstrated ownership of data quality: validation frameworks, data contracts, tests, and alerting on the data itself — not only on job success. Experience with observability and dashboarding tooling (Prometheus/Grafana, Superset, Tableau or similar). Cloud engineering on Alibaba Cloud (Aliyun) and/or AWS, including object storage at scale, with Terraform as the primary IaC tool. Docker and Kubernetes for building and running data workloads. Solid cost awareness: able to reason about storage tiering, egress and compute trade-offs and act on them. Soft Skills Must-haves Strong ownership; treats data correctness as a product commitment rather than a ticket queue. Clear communicator who can turn a vague analytical question into a concrete dataset or metric definition. Collaborative across autonomy, ML, backend and operations teams. Pragmatic prioritization: knows when to ship a pipeline and when to fix the foundation underneath it. Comfortable inheriting, understanding and improving existing systems, not only greenfield builds. Willingness to be on-call for data platform issues. Nice-to-haves Experience with robotics or vehicle data: ROS 2 bags, MCAP, time-series sensor data and geospatial data. Familiarity with visualization tooling for robotics data (Foxglove or similar). ML platform experience: feature stores, training pipelines, experiment tracking and model registries (Kubeflow, MLflow or equivalent). Experience with large-scale multimedia data (camera, lidar) indexing and retrieval. Exposure to simulation-generated datasets — we primarily use CARLA and NVIDIA Isaac Sim. Experience operating data infrastructure across multiple regions, including cross-border data constraints. Experience working across APAC sites or with distributed, cross-border teams.
