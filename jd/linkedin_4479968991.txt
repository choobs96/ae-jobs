About the Role We are looking for a motivated Data Platform Engineer to join Data Engineering team in Singapore. The team designs, operates, and enhances an enterprise-scale Data Lake and Data Platform supporting analytics, reporting, machine learning, enterprise search, and AI-driven solutions across cloud and on-premises environments. The successful candidate will build and maintain scalable, secure, and reliable data pipelines and platform services, with a strong focus on AWS, Python, PySpark, SQL, automation, and production support. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain scalable data platform solutions across AWS and hybrid/on-premises environments. Build, optimize, and support production-grade data pipelines using Python, PySpark, SQL, and cloud-native technologies. Implement and maintain ETL/ELT workflows, Data Lake/Lakehouse architectures, metadata management, search platforms, and AI services. Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines, automated testing frameworks, deployment workflows, and infrastructure automation. Implement, integrate, and support AWS services and technologies such as OpenSearch, Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, MCP, and related platform components. Monitor and optimize platform performance, reliability, security, observability, and cost efficiency. Troubleshoot production incidents, perform root-cause analysis, and ensure platform stability and operational excellence. Collaborate with data engineering, infrastructure, security, and application teams to drive platform modernisation and continuous improvement. Mandatory Requirements 3–5+ years of hands-on experience building and supporting production-grade data pipelines using SQL, Python, and cloud technologies. Practical experience with AWS and/or hybrid cloud environments. Strong Linux/Unix administration, shell scripting, troubleshooting, and production support capabilities. Solid understanding of data engineering principles, ETL/ELT processes, and distributed data processing. Technical Skills Programming & Data Processing: Python, PySpark, SQL, Apache Spark AWS & Data Platforms: AWS Glue, EMR, S3, Redshift, Lambda Data Engineering & Streaming: Airflow, dbt, Kafka/MSK, Apache Iceberg, Hive, Trino, Presto Search & AI Platforms: OpenSearch, Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, MCP DevOps & Automation: Git, CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), automated testing, deployment automation Monitoring & Operations: Datadog, CloudWatch, Grafana, Prometheus, Linux/Unix administration Preferred Qualifications Experience with enterprise Data Lake and Lakehouse platforms. Knowledge of streaming and event-driven architectures. Hands-on experience with OpenSearch administration, performance optimisation, and tuning. Familiarity with monitoring, observability, and production support tools. Understanding of data governance, security, access controls, and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) protection. Experience in banking, insurance, or other financial services environments.
