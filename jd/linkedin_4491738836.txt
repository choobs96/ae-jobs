About Rimes Rimes provides the Intelligence Fabric for Capital Markets, a trusted data network and intelligence architecture that transforms fragmented data, operations and workflows into decision-grade intelligence. The world's leading institutional investors, asset managers, and service providers rely on Rimes to help them make better investment decisions that power more than US$ 75 trillion in AUM annually. The Opportunity The Data Team at Rimes delivers financial datasets that support critical investment decisions for institutional clients globally. As a Data Developer, you will help maintain, enhance, and support the data solutions that power Rimes' enterprise data products. You will work with large-scale and complex financial datasets, implementing changes, resolving data issues, and improving the quality and reliability of our data services. This role is well suited to someone who enjoys solving data problems, writing reliable code, and taking ownership of solutions in production. Responsibilities : Maintain and enhance production data solutions using technologies including Python, PySpark, SQL, and Perl to ingest, transform, validate, and deliver financial datasets. Implement changes to existing data services, including vendor feed, schema, methodology, content, and client-driven requirements. Translate business, product, client, and data requirements into reliable and maintainable technical solutions. Investigate and resolve data quality, processing, and performance issues, taking ownership from diagnosis through to resolution. Develop and maintain quality controls that improve the accuracy, consistency, and reliability of data delivery. Improve existing data solutions through automation, simplification, technical remediation, and performance optimization. Support feed transitions, historical data requests, reprocessing, data remediation, and client-requested supplementation. Support the introduction of new capabilities into established production services. Contribute source, product, and data expertise to migration and transformation initiatives. Develop monitoring and tooling that improve operational resilience and reduce manual intervention. Participate in code reviews, testing, documentation, release processes, and established software development practices. Collaborate with Product, Data Operations, Client Experience, Professional Services, Platform Engineering, and other teams to deliver high-quality data solutions. Build knowledge of financial markets, investment data, and the datasets supported by Rimes. Requirements: Strong programming skills in Python, with experience in PySpark or other data-processing frameworks considered valuable. Experience with SQL and working with structured or semi-structured datasets. Experience with Perl, or a willingness to develop Perl skills. Experience with Git or a similar version-control system. Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent practical experience. Experience in data development, data engineering, software development, or another technical role involving data processing. Experience developing, maintaining, or supporting production data solutions. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate complex data issues methodically. Strong attention to detail, with a focus on data quality and reliability. Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities effectively. Clear written and verbal communication skills. A proactive and collaborative approach, with a willingness to learn and take increasing ownership. Preferred Experience: Hands-on experience with Databricks or other distributed data-processing platforms. Experience working with REST APIs and data formats such as XML, JSON, and Parquet. Experience maintaining or supporting production ETL/ELT pipelines. Understanding of financial markets or financial datasets, including reference data, index data, pricing data, performance data, or investment analytics. Experience working with complex vendor datasets and implementing vendor-driven changes. Experience with historical data loading, reprocessing, reconciliation, or feed transitions. Familiarity with modern data platforms such as Snowflake or Palantir Foundry. Experience using Excel for data investigation, reconciliation, or validation. Progress toward CFA or another relevant financial-markets qualification. What We Offer: Competitive compensation and bonus eligibility. Flexible hybrid working environment. Opportunities to develop technical expertise across a range of data technologies. Exposure to complex financial datasets and the global investment data ecosystem. Opportunities to develop deep product and financial-data expertise. Opportunities to grow into greater technical ownership and more senior Data Development responsibilities.
