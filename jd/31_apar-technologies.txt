Data Engineer (Databricks / PySpark)
Key Responsibilities
 Develop and maintain data pipelines using PySpark and Databricks.
 Process and transform data using Delta Lake and SQL.
 Optimize pipelines for performance and reliability.
 Integrate data from APIs, databases, and files.
 Implement data quality checks and monitor pipelines.
 Collaborate with analysts and business teams to meet data requirements.
 Requirements
 3–5 years of relevant data engineering experience.
 Strong knowledge of Python, PySpark, and SQL.
 Hands-on experience with Databricks and Delta Lake.
 Experience with Azure, AWS, or GCP.
 Knowledge of data modelling, ETL/ELT, Git, and CI/CD.
 Preferred Skills
 Databricks certification.
 Experience with Unity Catalog, Airflow, or Azure Data Factory.
 Knowledge of Kafka, Terraform, or dbt.
