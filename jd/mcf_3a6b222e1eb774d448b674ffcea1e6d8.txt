  My Client:   My client is a fast-growing global technology company with an established international business spanning smart hardware, mobile applications, and e-commerce. The company is building its Singapore data team to support global operations. The team develops reliable data pipelines, standardized data models, and reusable data products that help business and engineering teams make decisions and build data-powered applications.   The Role:   The team is hiring Data Engineers at different seniority levels to develop and maintain data pipelines and own key business data domains.  You will work closely with business stakeholders, analysts, product managers, and engineers to turn data from multiple systems and regions into trusted, reusable datasets.   Job Responsibilities:    Design, build, and maintain batch and streaming data pipelines for analytics and business applications.  Develop standardized detail layers, shared dimensions, domain models, aggregates, and reusable data products.  Translate business requirements into clear data definitions, transformation logic, data contracts, and service levels.  Implement automated tests, data quality checks, monitoring, and source reconciliation to ensure data accuracy and completeness.  Improve pipeline performance, reliability, and cost efficiency, and troubleshoot production data issues.  Support controlled data publishing across regions and resolve downstream data discrepancies.  Maintain technical documentation and contribute to standards for data modeling, code quality, and operational practices.    Job Requirements:    Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.  Experience in data engineering, analytics engineering, or data warehousing.  Strong proficiency in SQL and Python, with production experience using Spark or comparable data processing technologies.  Solid understanding of dimensional modeling, data warehouse layering, incremental processing, and workflow orchestration.  Experience implementing data quality controls and maintaining reliable production data pipelines.  Ability to manage the full data lifecycle, from source integration and transformation to downstream delivery.  Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with distributed business and technical teams.  Experience with Databricks, dbt, MetricFlow, or other semantic-layer technologies is an advantage.  Experience with commerce, advertising, order, user, or consumer data is preferred.  Exposure to cross-region data publishing, de-identified data, or controlled datasets is a plus.    Why Join:    Join the early Singapore data team and contribute to its engineering foundations and standards.  Own business-critical data domains supporting an established and growing global business.  Gain exposure to cross-region data engineering, governance, and collaboration with an established HQ technical team.
