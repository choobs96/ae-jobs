Role Overview Role Title: Senior Business Data Product Manager Function: Data Seniority Level: Mid-Senior level Employment Type: Full time Core Responsibilities Business Partnership & Strategy: Embed within core business lines (e.g., Hotel/Car Rental/Marketing/Platform) as its data strategy partner. Diagnose business pains and plan the data product roadmap for that domain. Team Leadership & Delivery Management: Lead a small data product delivery team (including Data Mart Analysts) to translate business requirements into concrete Data Mart designs, reports, and dashboards, ensuring high-quality and timely delivery. Drive Business Value & Enablement: Own the business impact of data products. Foster a data-driven culture through training, review, and impact tracking. Quantify value contributions (e.g., revenue growth, efficiency gain, cost optimization). Qualifications Business & Data Expertise: 5+ years in data product or BI roles, with at least 2 years deeply embedded in a business line (e.g., transactional platform, growth marketing, supply chain). Excellent business diagnosis, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence mid-to-senior business leaders. Proficient in metric design and data modeling, capable of leading complex Data Mart design. Strong SQL skills. Hands-on experience with BigQuery + DBT is a strong plus. Team Management & Delivery Skills: Proven experience in leading analysts or a small data product team, skilled in task breakdown, prioritization, and cross-functional project execution. Good sense of data visualization and reporting UX, able to guide the team to produce clear, actionable data products. Language & Soft Skills: Fluent verbal and written communication skills in both English and Chinese. Self-motivated, result-oriented, with strong project drive and collaboration skills.
