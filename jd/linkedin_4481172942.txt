The successful candidate will possess deep expertise in stakeholder management, project and program delivery, self-service analytics, dashboarding, data governance, and end-to-end analytics implementation. The role requires a strong blend of business consulting, analytics leadership, and hands-on delivery capabilities to transform complex business challenges into scalable analytics solutions that create measurable business impact.
