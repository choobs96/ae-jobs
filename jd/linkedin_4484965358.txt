Job Overview We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to help us architecting, implementing and operating the complete data infrastructure pipeline for our Research and Trading operations. This role will be crucial in building a scalable, reliable, and cost-efficient system for handling vast amounts of market trading data, real-time news feeds and a variety of internal and external data sources. The ideal candidate will be a hands-on professional who understands the entire data lifecycle and can drive innovation while collaborating across research and engineering teams to meet their needs. Create tools for overlaying data from multiple sources Ensure that data storage, processing, and management are done in a cost-effective manner, optimizing both hardware and software resources.
