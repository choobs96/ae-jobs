The opportunity As Vice President, Software Engineer (Data), you will lead engineering across the data domain, spanning the applications, data platforms and data products that SGX Group's business and market infrastructure depends on. You will set direction, strengthen engineering discipline and take accountability for how data services, and the applications and interfaces built on top of them, are designed, delivered, operated and improved. The challenge is to increase agility without compromising resilience, security, governance or operational reliability. In a mission-critical environment where data carries regulatory, client and market obligations, engineering decisions have real consequences, and you will need to make sound trade-offs across architecture, delivery, risk and business priorities. There are strong foundations to build on, and a significant amount still to do. The engineering model, the data platform, tooling and ways of working will keep changing. If you want to inherit a mature data engineering organisation, this is not that one yet. It will suit someone who enjoys complexity, is comfortable leading through change and wants to build capability that lasts. The Data Engineering Team This role performs software engineering within the Data Engineering team that builds, runs and evolves the applications, data platforms and data products behind SGX Group’s business and market infrastructure. The team works closely with product, architecture, platform, data governance, analytics, security and delivery colleagues to turn business priorities into secure, resilient and scalable data services. Its remit spans the full engineering lifecycle, from technical design, build, data modelling, CICD, testing etc through to production ownership of pipelines and continuous improvement. Our next phase is about strengthening engineering ownership, modernising our data platform and practices, treating data as a product, and building consistency across teams without flattening the different needs of each business domain. What you will do Software Development: Lead development of complex applications, services and APIs across the data domain, establish coding standards and mentor engineers, including the user-facing products that extend the data platform, data-as-a-service services and data agents etc. Software Design: Lead design of major systems and data architectures and influence cross-domain technical design, spanning application, API, storage, processing and data serving layers. Data Product Engineering: Set the direction for how data products are built and operated — curated, documented, contract-backed datasets and data services — and establish the standards that make them discoverable, trusted and usable by consumers across the organisation and beyond. Data Platform Engineering: Lead the evolution of shared data platform capabilities spanning ingestion, storage, processing, orchestration, cataloguing and self-service tooling, so teams across SGX Group can deliver data capabilities safely and at speed. Data Pipeline Development: Lead the design of production-grade batch and streaming pipelines and establish the quality, performance and security controls applied across them. Data Quality & Reliability: Establish quality engineering practices for both applications and data, drive defect prevention initiatives, and define service levels for freshness, completeness and accuracy. Secure Software & Data Development: Drive security-by-design practices across teams and ensure sensitive market, client and reference data is handled in line with standards. Data Governance & Compliance: Partner with data governance to set lineage, cataloguing, entitlements, access control, retention and privacy practices in line with regulatory and internal obligations, and ensure they are surfaced through the tooling that data users interact with. System & Data Integration: Lead integration design across the data platform and trading, clearing, data services, market operations, risk and enterprise systems. SDLC & Delivery Excellence: Lead adoption of SDLC best practices, automation and engineering standards, applying the same engineering rigour to data assets as to application code. Observability & Production Support: Lead reliability improvements across applications, pipelines and data services through telemetry, monitoring and operational practices, including incident leadership. Automation & Productivity: Lead engineering productivity improvements through platform and workflow optimisation, including self-service capabilities that reduce manual handling of data requests. Leadership & Mentoring: Lead engineering communities and develop technical talent across software and data engineering practice. Agentic AI Engineering: Lead the adoption of agentic AI engineering practices in the domain, define usage patterns and guardrails, mentor engineers on agent orchestration, and improve shared agent workflows so recurring defects, review gaps and testing weaknesses are addressed through reusable automation. Set direction for the governed data foundations that make analytics and AI workloads viable. Translate enterprise, technology and data strategy into actionable engineering plans, partnering closely with project and programme leaders to align priorities, resources, delivery milestones, and management reporting. Lead engineering, data and architecture governance forums, and engage senior stakeholders, including internal and external data consumers, on strategic priorities, trade-offs, delivery progress, and investment decisions.
