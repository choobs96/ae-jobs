As a Senior Data Engineer III, you will play a critical role in ensuring the stability, resilience, and reliability of production data platforms and products across the Capital Group data ecosystem. You will focus on building and operating monitoring, automation, and observability capabilities that enable early detection of issues, rapid resolution, and continuous service improvement. You will partner closely with data product, engineering, and platform teams to support production services while creating strong feedback loops that translate operational insights into improved engineering standards, system design, and platform reliability. You bring an operations-first mindset, a passion for continuous improvement, and a strong sense of ownership for production stability. "I am the person Capital Group is looking for." Production Operations & Reliability You own the operational health, availability, and reliability of production data platforms and pipelines. You design and implement monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions to enable early detection of issues. You lead incident response, triage, and resolution efforts, restoring services quickly and safely. You conduct root cause analysis and postmortems, driving corrective actions to prevent recurrence. You proactively identify risks, trends, and service gaps, and implement improvements to strengthen resilience. You define and track SLAs/SLOs and continuously improve service performance. Monitoring, Data Quality & Operational Controls You build and maintain end-to-end monitoring capabilities across pipelines, data quality, and delivery layers. You define and implement data quality rules, controls, and validation frameworks. You ensure visibility into pipeline health, data freshness, completeness, and accuracy. You automate validation and health checks to reduce manual effort and improve reliability. Production Support of Data Platforms & Products You support and optimize existing data pipelines and platforms, ensuring operability, scalability, and performance. You partner with engineering teams to ensure solutions are production-ready with appropriate monitoring and support models. You contribute to operational readiness for new data products. You create and maintain runbooks, documentation, and support procedures. Continuous Improvement & Engineering Feedback You establish strong feedback loops between production operations and engineering teams. You translate incidents and operational trends into actionable improvements and engineering standards. You influence system design by advocating for resilience, observability, and supportability. You promote automation, standardization, and reliability-first practices. Collaboration & Leadership You collaborate with product, platform, governance, and business stakeholders to align operational priorities with business outcomes. You represent reliability and support considerations in design and roadmap discussions. You mentor junior engineers and promote a culture of operational excellence.
