  Senior Data Engineer – AWS / Databricks / PySpark   Role Overview  We are seeking an experienced  Senior Data Engineer  to design, develop and maintain scalable data pipelines and reusable data products on an AWS and Databricks-based data platform. The role involves technical leadership in data engineering, lakehouse architecture, data quality, governance and performance optimisation to support analytics, reporting and AI/ML workloads.  Key Responsibilities    Design, develop and optimise scalable batch and streaming data pipelines using  AWS, Databricks, Python, PySpark and SQL .    Build and maintain lakehouse architectures using  Delta Lake , including Bronze, Silver and Gold data layers, curated datasets and reusable data products.    Implement data ingestion from databases, APIs, files and event streams, including incremental loading, Change Data Capture (CDC), backfills and recovery.    Establish automated data quality checks, schema validation, reconciliation, metadata management and data lineage.    Develop and automate data workflows using  Databricks Workflows, Lakeflow and AWS services , incorporating CI/CD, automated testing and infrastructure as code.    Implement data governance, access controls and security measures using  Unity Catalog, AWS Glue Data Catalog, Lake Formation and IAM .    Monitor pipeline performance, troubleshoot production issues and optimise Spark/SQL workloads for scalability, reliability and cost efficiency.    Lead technical design and code reviews, establish engineering standards and mentor junior engineers.    Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, architects and business stakeholders to deliver reliable data solutions.    Maintain technical documentation, data models, operational procedures and knowledge transfer materials.    Requirements    Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Engineering, Information Systems or a related discipline.     6–10 years of data/software engineering experience , including at least 2 years working with cloud-based data platforms.    Strong hands-on experience with  Python, PySpark, SQL, Apache Spark, Databricks and Delta Lake .    Proficiency in AWS data services such as  S3, Glue, Lake Formation, Lambda, Step Functions, EventBridge, CloudWatch, Redshift, MSK/Kinesis, IAM and KMS .    Experience designing data lake/lakehouse architectures, data models and production-grade batch or streaming pipelines.    Strong understanding of  ETL/ELT, CDC, incremental ingestion, data quality, schema validation, metadata and lineage .    Experience with workflow orchestration, Git, CI/CD, automated testing and infrastructure as code, preferably  Terraform .    Knowledge of data pipeline monitoring, observability, troubleshooting and performance optimisation.    Familiarity with cloud security, encryption, access management and data governance.    Proven ability to provide technical guidance, conduct code reviews and mentor engineers.    Good to Have    Experience with  Databricks Unity Catalog, Lakeflow, Delta Live Tables, Auto Loader, Structured Streaming, Delta Sharing and Databricks Asset Bundles .    Familiarity with  Apache Airflow, dbt, Kafka, AWS MSK/Kinesis and AWS DMS .    Knowledge of data contracts, data products, semantic layers, master/reference data and data mesh concepts.    Experience supporting ML/AI data pipelines, feature engineering, feature stores or MLOps.    Exposure to Singapore public-sector environments, including  IM8, VAPT, data classification and cross-agency data governance .
