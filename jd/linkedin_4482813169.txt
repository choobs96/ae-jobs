Senior Data Engineers Roles & Responsibilities What you will be working on We are looking for Senior Data Engineers to design, build, and scale enterprise data platforms and pipelines. You will shape data architecture, drive data integration and quality, and develop scalable solutions to support analytics, business decisions, and AI-enabled applications. Design data architectures, data models, and scalable data pipelines using ETL/ELT, APIs, and batch or streaming integrations. Build and maintain data platforms, including data lakes, data warehouses, and data integration frameworks. Develop data products, analytics workflows, and AI-enabled solutions using machine learning and LLM technologies where relevant. Optimise pipeline performance, data quality, reliability, and system scalability. Implement DataOps practices, automation, monitoring, and CI/CD for data pipelines. Establish data governance, security, privacy, and quality standards. Work closely with product, engineering, business, and infrastructure teams to translate requirements into technical solutions. Lead technical discussions, review architectures, mentor engineers, and drive delivery best practices. What we are looking for At least 7-8 years of experience in data engineering, data architecture, or building enterprise data platforms. Strong data modelling skills across OLTP, OLAP, and dimensional modelling. Experience with cloud data platforms and services such as AWS, Glue, Redshift, Snowflake, Databricks, or Azure. Proficiency in Python, Scala, or Java, with experience in SQL and ETL/ELT development. Familiarity with data lakes, data warehouses, APIs, streaming, CDC, or message queues. Experience with CI/CD, DataOps, data observability, and automated data quality checks. Understanding of AI/ML and LLM-based applications or MLOps is an advantage. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills. Ability to take ownership of complex technical assignments and work effectively across teams. Good to have: Experience in large-scale platform modernisation, complex data integrations, and AI-enabled data solutions.
