Role Overview Role Title: Data Mart Analyst Function: Data Seniority Level: Associate Employment Type: Full time Core Responsibilities Requirement Implementation & Data Development: Based on Data Mart design documents and business requirements from Data Product Managers, efficiently and accurately develop ADS-layer data models, reports, and dashboards using SQL and data analysis tools. Data Validation & Quality Assurance: Conduct rigorous data validation on developed datasets and reports to ensure accurate metric calculation, consistent definitions, and reliable data. Document related processes. Product Operations & User Support: Responsible for the daily monitoring, maintenance, and performance tuning of live data products. Provide frontline user support, basic training, and gather user feedback. Efficiency Tool Utilization: Proficiently use the team's data toolstack (e.g., DBT for transformation, BigQuery for querying and processing, and BI tools like Tableau/Looker for dashboard creation) to enhance development efficiency and quality. Qualifications Must-Have: 2+ years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence (BI), or data development. Expert proficiency in SQL, with excellent data processing, logical structuring, and performance tuning skills. Hands-on experience in developing complex reports and dashboards using modern BI tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, Looker, QuickSight). Strong data sensitivity and attention to detail, capable of independently ensuring the accuracy of data deliverables. Nice-to-Have: Experience with DBT and BigQuery, familiar with their development paradigms. Experience working in agile teams and with project management tools like Jira and Confluence. Basic business acumen with the ability to quickly learn common metrics in specific domains (e.g., e-commerce, OTA).
