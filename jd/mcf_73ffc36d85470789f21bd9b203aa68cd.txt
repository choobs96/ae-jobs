 Job Summary:  We are seeking a  Data Engineer to support the development and modernization of an enterprise data and analytics platform . The successful candidate will design, build and maintain reliable, well-tested data pipelines and dimensional data models that turn raw data into trusted, reusable datasets for business users. You will bring strong hands-on SQL and Python skills, practical experience with dbt (or an equivalent transformation framework) and workflow orchestration, and a disciplined approach to modern software engineering practices. This is a  12-months contract based  in Central Singapore.     Mandatory Skills:    Minimum 3 to 6 years of relevant experience in Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, Data Warehousing or a related field;  Must have strong hands-on experience with SQL, including complex queries and performance tuning;  Must have working knowledge of Python for data processing and automation;  Proven experience in dimensional data modelling, including star schemas, fact tables, dimension tables and data marts;  Hands-on experience with dbt or an equivalent data transformation framework;  Experience with at least one workflow orchestration tool, such as Airflow or equivalent;  Understanding of semantic layers, common business metrics and reusable business logic;  Working knowledge of software development best practices: Git/version control, code reviews, unit and data testing, and CI/CD;  Good understanding of ETL/ELT patterns and data warehouse concepts;  Ability to troubleshoot data and pipeline issues independently and find root causes;  Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work with both technical and business stakeholders.   Responsibilities:   Design, build and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines that support the modernisation of the data and analytics platform;  Develop and maintain dimensional data models (star schemas, facts, dimensions and data marts) that serve analytics and reporting needs;  Build modular, documented and reusable transformations using dbt or an equivalent framework;  Schedule, monitor and orchestrate data workflows using Airflow or an equivalent tool, ensuring reliability and timely delivery;  Define and maintain semantic layers and standard business metrics so that business logic is consistent and reusable across teams;  Review existing business logic, including legacy .NET processes, and assist in migrating or redesigning it on cloud-native data services;  Apply engineering best practices: version control, peer code reviews, automated unit and data-quality tests, and CI/CD deployment;  Investigate and resolve data quality issues and pipeline failures, and implement preventive controls;  Partner with analysts, BI developers and business stakeholders to understand requirements and ensure datasets are fit for consumption;  Maintain clear documentation of data models, lineage and pipeline logic to support ongoing platform maintenance.
