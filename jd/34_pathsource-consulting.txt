Senior Big Data Engineer (Compute & Transformation) – Contract
The client is a Singapore-based data consultancy seeking a hands-on Senior Infrastructure Engineer to deploy and operate the compute and transformation layer of a large-scale on-premise data platform.
 This is an initial contract position, with the potential for extension or conversion to a permanent position based on business requirements and individual performance.
 Key Responsibilities
 · Design, deploy and operate distributed compute, query and transformation services in an on-premise or hybrid environment.
 · Containerise platform components using Docker and deploy and operate production workloads on Kubernetes.
 · Build and support large-scale processing and query services using Spark, Trino and ClickHouse.
 · Develop and maintain governed transformation workflows using dbt.
 · Integrate compute services with Apache Iceberg tables and Apache Polaris catalogues.
 · Tune workload performance, resource allocation, concurrency and reliability for terabyte- or petabyte-scale data.
 · Implement monitoring and logging using VictoriaMetrics and VictoriaLogs or comparable observability tools.
 · Troubleshoot distributed-system, workload, storage-integration and production-performance issues.
 · Produce technical documentation, operating procedures and deployment runbooks.
 · Work directly with technical stakeholders and contribute as a senior individual contributor.
 Requirements
 · Minimum six years of hands-on experience in data-platform, big-data infrastructure, distributed systems or a closely related role.
 · Production experience with both Docker and Kubernetes is mandatory.
 · Strong experience deploying and operating distributed data workloads in on-premise or hybrid environments.
 · Hands-on experience with distributed compute and query technologies such as Spark, Trino and ClickHouse.
 · Practical experience with data transformation and lakehouse technologies including dbt, Apache Iceberg and Apache Polaris.
 · Experience monitoring platform workloads through VictoriaMetrics, VictoriaLogs or comparable technologies.
 · Strong understanding of Linux, networking, resource management, performance tuning, high availability and recovery.
 · Experience supporting high-volume platforms at terabyte or petabyte scale.
 · Ability to operate independently and communicate effectively with client and technical stakeholders.
 · Willingness to accept a six-to-twelve-month contract and attend client locations when required.
 Preferred Experience
 · Data-platform delivery within regulated sectors such as banking, fintech, healthcare or data centres.
 · Experience with Kubernetes Operators, Helm, CI/CD and infrastructure automation.
 · Experience integrating compute engines with distributed storage such as Ceph.
 This position focuses on the compute and transformation infrastructure of a data platform. It is not primarily a business-intelligence, dashboarding, visualisation or data-analyst role.
