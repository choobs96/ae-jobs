About The Role As a Data Architect / Modeler, you will design scalable, governed data solutions on Databricks and translate complex business requirements into practical target-state architectures. You will combine hands-on data modeling with architecture ownership, working across business, architecture, governance, and engineering teams to establish reusable modern data practices. Key Responsibilities Define conceptual, logical, and physical data models aligned with Databricks lakehouse architecture and enterprise standards. Translate business requirements, definitions, and rules into data models, mappings, and implementable designs. Assess source systems, data structures, dependencies, and flows to define effective target-state architectures. Design solutions using Databricks, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, and medallion architecture patterns. Establish reusable data architecture standards, modeling conventions, and design principles. Partner with data engineering teams to validate designs, resolve architecture gaps, and guide implementation. Embed data governance, metadata, lineage, data quality, security, and standardization into solution designs. Lead design reviews and maintain requirements-to-data traceability across delivery initiatives. Required Qualifications 5+ years of hands-on experience in data architecture, data modeling, data engineering, or related disciplines. Strong experience developing conceptual, logical, and physical data models from complex business requirements. Hands-on knowledge of Databricks, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, and medallion architecture. Strong understanding of enterprise data architecture, integration patterns, dimensional modeling, normalized schemas, and data design standards. Proficiency in data modeling techniques such as ERD, UML, and dimensional modeling. Experience incorporating data governance, metadata, lineage, and data quality requirements into solution design. Strong stakeholder-management and communication skills across technical and business audiences. Preferred Qualifications Production experience designing data models for Databricks lakehouse environments. Knowledge of Delta Lake optimization, partitioning, Databricks SQL, Spark SQL, and performance tuning. Experience with banking or financial-services data models and large-scale cloud or data transformation initiatives. Knowledge of Data Vault, advanced dimensional modeling, or complex normalized enterprise models. Familiarity with metadata, lineage, and data quality platforms such as OpenMetadata. Relevant data architecture, cloud, or Databricks certifications.
