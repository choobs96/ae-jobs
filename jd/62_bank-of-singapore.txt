We are seeking an experienced Power BI Developer to manage and govern the bank’s enterprise Power BI platform, with responsibility for Power BI Service running on Microsoft Fabric capacity and the controlled deployment of Power BI reports.
The role will ensure that Power BI solutions are developed, reviewed, deployed, monitored, and maintained in line with enterprise best practices, internal governance standards, security requirements, and operational controls expected in a regulated banking environment.
The candidate will act as the technical lead for Power BI development practices, platform governance, performance optimization, release management, and Python-script based deployment automation for reports published to the bank’s Power BI environments. The role will also require strong hands-on SQL capability to support data sourced from various platforms, including Starburst, AWS data services, and other enterprise data platforms.
Key Responsibilities
 Govern and maintain the bank’s enterprise Power BI platform, including Power BI Service, Microsoft Fabric capacity, workspaces, gateways, access controls, and platform standards.
 Lead Power BI development practices and ensure reports, semantic models, dataflows, and dashboards are designed, reviewed, and delivered in line with enterprise best practices.
 Write, review, and optimize SQL scripts supporting Power BI solutions, especially for data sourced from Starburst/Trino, AWS data platforms, on-premises databases, and other enterprise data sources.
 Maintain controlled report deployment processes, including Python-based automation, Jenkins-based deployment workflows, release coordination, version control alignment, and operational support.
 Monitor and improve platform reliability, refresh performance, report responsiveness, capacity consumption, and overall user experience.
 Support data platform changes, migrations, and integration activities that impact Power BI reporting, ensuring continuity, performance, and compliance with internal controls.
 Partner with business users, data engineering, infrastructure, security, and governance teams to promote secure, scalable, and well-managed analytics delivery.
