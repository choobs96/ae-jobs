About The Role We are looking for a curious, hands-on and self-motivated individual to join the Group Finance Systems & Analytics team. In this role, you will help build and enhance the Group's Finance data, analytics and AI capabilities, with a particular focus on leveraging Google Cloud Platform (GCP), BigQuery and Generative AI technologies to enhance reporting, analytics and business decision-making. You will work alongside Finance subject matter experts, technology teams and business stakeholders to develop data products, reporting solutions, automation initiatives and AI-enabled use cases that improve decision-making and business performance. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with no direct people management responsibilities. We are looking for a strong team player who enjoys rolling up their sleeves, building practical solutions, experimenting with new technologies and continuously learning. This role provides an exciting opportunity to gain exposure to enterprise-scale data platforms, cloud analytics, finance transformation programmes and real-world AI applications within a leading multinational real estate group. How You’ll Make An Impact Finance Data Platform & Analytics Develop and maintain Finance datasets within the Group Data Lake environment. Build and enhance data pipelines, transformations and reporting datasets. Support data integration across Finance, ERP and operational systems. Perform data validation, reconciliation and quality checks to ensure data accuracy and reliability. Collaborate with Finance stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into practical data and analytics solutions. Data Modelling & Reporting Build and maintain business-ready data models to support financial reporting, management reporting, planning and performance analysis. Develop and enhance KPI calculations, reporting logic and data transformations using SQL and analytics tools. Support the standardisation of key metrics and reporting definitions across the Group. Work closely with Finance stakeholders, technology teams and vendors to define reporting requirements, shape user experience and drive the delivery of intuitive dashboards, visualisations and analytics solutions that support business decision-making. AI & Intelligent Automation Support the development and implementation of AI-enabled solutions and automation initiatives within Finance. Build and maintain datasets and knowledge repositories that support AI use cases. Participate in Generative AI proof-of-concepts and pilot projects. Evaluate emerging AI technologies and identify opportunities to improve reporting, analytics and business processes. Collaboration & Continuous Improvement Work closely with Finance, Group Digital Technology and external partners on data and AI initiatives. Support the delivery and adoption of data, analytics and AI solutions. Continuously learn and apply new technologies to improve Finance processes and business outcomes. What You’ll Bring Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Engineering, Accountancy, Finance or a related discipline. Approximately 2-5 years of relevant experience in data analytics, business intelligence, analytics engineering, data engineering or related roles. Candidates with less experience but strong technical aptitude, relevant project experience and a demonstrated interest in Data, Analytics and AI are encouraged to apply. Strong SQL skills and experience working with structured datasets. Experience using Python or similar technologies for data analysis, automation or data transformation. Experience or exposure to Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Google BigQuery through work experience, project delivery, academic projects or self-learning initiatives. Experience working with business and technology teams to deliver reporting, dashboarding or analytics solutions. Strong analytical, problem-solving and communication skills. Ability to work effectively with both business and technical stakeholders. Self-motivated with a strong learning mindset and willingness to explore new technologies. Exposure to Finance, FP&A, management reporting, ERP/EPM platforms, Generative AI technologies, data transformation initiatives or the real estate industry will be advantageous. Candidates who are assessed to be more experienced may be considered for a Senior Manager role.
