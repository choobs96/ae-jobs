[What you will be working on] Who we are The mission of GovTech's Data Programme is to deliver fit-for-purpose data assets and services for policy making, service delivery and operations across Whole-of-Government (WOG). Our programme creates value through enablement - reducing friction for agencies so they can move faster and make better decisions in their work with existing government datasets. Key Technical Responsibilities: You will contribute to the development of one or more of our four strategic technical initiatives: Standardised Data Platform Build and maintain unified data platform infrastructure for government agencies Develop APIs and services that standardise data ingestion, processing, and storage Create reusable components and templates for agency data platform implementation Implement data connectors and integration tools for legacy systems Unified Frontend / DataHive Develop the central data discovery and access request platform Build intuitive user interfaces for data catalogue browsing and metadata search Implement request management workflows and approval systems Create responsive web applications that serve both data consumers and providers Unified Serving Backbone Design and implement standardised data delivery APIs and microservices Build robust data serving infrastructure that can handle high-volume requests Develop authentication and authorisation systems for secure data access Create monitoring and alerting systems for data delivery pipelines Observability & Governance Infrastructure Build central observability systems for data quality monitoring and lineage tracking Develop dashboards and analytics tools for data usage patterns Implement automated data quality checks and validation frameworks Create audit trails and compliance reporting systems What the role is Role You will work on both small and large scale projects, building and maintaining the infrastructure behind them. We are fully aligned with the Government's cloud-first policy and you will bring capability to help us realize this. The role includes: Managing the development, deployment, orchestration and maintenance of data pipelines for our products Ensuring the scalability, performance, and reliability of Cloud-based applications Providing DevOps architecture implementation and operational support Architecture and planning for cloud deployments (Private and Public cloud) Developing and managing processes, automation, best practices, documentation Development and operation of continuous integration and deployment pipelines. What it is like working here We build products that serve a variety of agency users, who use them to solve highly meaningful problems pertinent to our society, from transportation, to education, to healthcare. The public sector is full of opportunities where even the simplest software can have a big impact on people’s lives. We are here to improve how we live as a society through what we can offer as a government. Rapid Prototyping - Instead of spending too much time debating ideas we prefer testing them. This identifies potential problems quickly, and more importantly, conveys what is possible to others easily. Reliable Productization - To scale an idea, a prototype or a Minimum Viable Product to a software product, we scrutinize and commit to its usability, reliability, scalability and maintainability. Ownership - In addition to technical responsibilities, this means having ideas on how things should be done and taking responsibility for seeing them through. Building something that you believe in is the best way to build something good. Continuous Learning - Working on new ideas often means not fully understanding what you are working on. Taking time to learn new architectures, frameworks, technologies, and even languages is not just encouraged but essential [What we are looking for] The Customers For Our Products Are Normally Agency Users, Which Means That Breadth Of Knowledge In Government IT Infrastructure And Experience In Government Networks Will Help.
