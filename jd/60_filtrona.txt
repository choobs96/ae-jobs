Senior Data Engineer
We are seeking a technically strong, hands-on Senior Data Engineer to build, develop, and optimize our enterprise data platform in a manufacturing environment. This role combines deep technical expertise in Microsoft Fabric, SAP CDS Views, MES, Salesforce, and Power BI / Power Automate. 
 You will be responsible for building scalable data pipelines, integrating IT and OT systems, and delivering trusted, high-performance analytics that drive operational efficiency, production visibility, and business performance.
 Key Responsibilities: 
 1. Data Engineering 
 Build and optimize scalable data platforms using Microsoft Fabric (Lakehouse, Data Factory, Warehouse, Real-Time Analytics)
 Develop and maintain robust ETL/ELT pipelines for batch and streaming data
 Optimize data storage, transformation, and query performance for cost optimisation
 Implement best practices in partitioning, indexing, and cost optimization
 2. BI Development & Analytics Delivery 
 Develop dashboards and reporting solutions using SQL, Power BI/Query/Automate and writing DAX formulas
 Ability to write Python for data manipulation and advanced analytics.
 Implement reusable semantic models and standardized datasets
 Enable self-service analytics while maintaining performance and governance
 Translate business requirements into technical data solutions
 4. Data Governance, Security & Quality 
 Ensure compliance with regulatory standards (e.g., GDPR, SOX where applicable)
 Enforce data standards, naming conventions, and access controls
 5. BAU & Reliability 
 Monitor pipelines with logging, alerting, and observability tools
 Troubleshoot and resolve performance bottlenecks and data issues
 Data quality monitoring and validation pipelines
 What we are looking for: 
 6+ years of hands-on experience in data engineering, BI, or analytics roles; in manufacturing environment preferred
 Strong expertise in Microsoft Fabric (Data Factory, Lakehouse, Warehouse, Real-Time Analytics) and Azure cloud platforms
 Proven experience with: SAP CDS Views; preferably with MES and Salesforce data integration and reporting experience in manufacturing environments 
 Advanced SQL and strong data modeling skills
 Hands-on experience with Power Query, Automate and Power BI
 Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Data Analytics or related field
 Strong communication, stakeholder management, and documentation skills
 Analytical mindset with attention to detail and problem-solving capability
 Experience with Agile delivery practice
 Prior experience managing vendors or external partners preferred
